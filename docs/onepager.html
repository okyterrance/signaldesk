Step 2 · How it works — the reader's side

Three commands, and one that runs itself

CommandWhat it does
/digestThe written briefing — arrives on its own at 08:30, or on demand
/topThe live ranking right now, with each story's score
/weightsReader controls: 5 subjects, and a depth setting
alertsRe-checks every 15 min; anything above 0.72 is pushed immediately

A briefing answers what happened yesterday.

One letter of English grammar. \b(exploit)\b cannot match “exploited”, so a $62m hack scored 0.00 on the heaviest factor. Every test was green throughout.

What I'd improve: the eight weights are reasoned, not fitted. Given more time I'd log every ranking against which stories readers open, and fit them — turning eight arguments into eight numbers.
